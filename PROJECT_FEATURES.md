
## T-02: Doctor Management

Description:
This feature manages doctor information in the Hospital Management System.

Functional Requirements:
- The system shall allow the administrator to add doctor information.
- The system shall allow the administrator to edit doctor information.
- The system shall allow the administrator to remove doctor information.
- The system shall allow users to view doctor schedules.

Status: Implemented


\### T-04 — Billing \& Payment Management



\*\*Short Description:\*\* Manage patient billing and payment information, including generating bills, recording payments, and tracking payment status.



\*\*Functional Requirements:\*\*

1\. The system shall generate and maintain bills for patients based on their services.

2\. The system shall record patient payment information and update the payment status.

3\. The system shall allow users to view billing details, including total amount, paid amount, and outstanding amount.
